# Job Preferences

## Role & Seniority
- Senior or mid/senior front-end engineer, front-end developer or React developer roles.
- Front-end tech lead roles with hands-on coding are a plus.
- Pure people-management roles with no coding are not a fit.

## Must-Haves
- React with TypeScript as the main front-end stack.
- Hybrid in Brussels or Charleroi with at most 3 office days per week, or fully remote
  within the EU.
- English or French as the working language.

## Disqualifiers
- Roles that are mainly back-end, DevOps or mobile-native development.
- Angular-only or Vue-only front ends with no React.
- Mandatory 24/7 on-call rotation.
- Gambling, weapons or adult-content industries.

## Nice-to-Haves
- Ownership of a design system or component library.
- Accessibility (WCAG) as an explicit part of the role.
- Next.js or server-side rendering.
- A product company rather than an agency or consultancy.

## Location
- Brussels, Belgium
- Charleroi, Belgium

## Home Address
Rue des Halles 4, 1000 Bruxelles, Belgium

## Scoring Notes
- A "full-stack" posting counts as front-end only when the front end is the stated focus of
  the role. When most of the listed responsibilities are back-end services, databases or
  infrastructure, score it as a back-end role even if React is mentioned.
- A lead or staff title with no hands-on front-end work described scores as a management
  role, not an engineering one.
- A consultancy or agency posting that places the engineer at changing client sites scores
  lower than an equivalent product-company role.

## Compensation & Logistics
- Gross salary of at least 65,000 EUR per year, or an equivalent daily rate as a contractor.
- Permanent contract preferred; freelance accepted for remote roles.
- Notice period of 2 months.
